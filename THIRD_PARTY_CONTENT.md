## Repository locations

Third-party puzzle material appears principally in:

- `src/App.tsx` — problem wording, tasks, hints, attribution, and explanatory reveals;
- `src/content/puzzles.ts` — puzzle datasets, tenji patterns, and answer data;
- `src/engine/*.test.ts` and `src/App.test.tsx` — puzzle-specific expected answers and test fixtures; and
- `docs/` — design notes that describe or reproduce parts of the selected problems.

The exclusion follows the third-party material, not merely these paths. Original interface code and project-authored analysis in the same files remain under MIT.

## Included adaptations

### Georgian Countries

- Author: Bruno L’Astorina
- Origin: Brazilian Linguistics Olympiad, 2016
- Republished in: IOL Sample Problems, Problem 1
- Official source: <https://ioling.org/booklets/samples.en.pdf>
- Material used: Georgian/English country-name dataset, task structure, answers, and an interactive explanation derived from the puzzle
- Permission status: request pending

### A Donkey in Every House

- Author: Todor Tchervenkov
- Origin: NACLO, 2007
- Republished in: IOL Sample Problems, Problem 2 (Ancient Greek)
- Official problem: <https://naclo.org/resources/problems/2007/N2007-C.pdf>
- Official solution: <https://naclo.org/resources/problems/2007/N2007-CS.pdf>
- Material used: Ancient Greek/English dataset, task structure, answers, hints, and an interactive explanation derived from the puzzle
- Permission status: request pending

### Tenji Karaoke

- Author: Patrick Littell
- Origin: NACLO, 2009
- Republished in: IOL Sample Problems, Problem 4 (Japanese Braille)
- Official problem: <https://naclo.org/resources/problems/2009/N2009-A.pdf>
- Official source archive: <https://naclo.org/practice.php>
- Material used: word list, tenji patterns, task structure, answers, hints, and an interactive explanation derived from the puzzle
- Permission status: request pending
